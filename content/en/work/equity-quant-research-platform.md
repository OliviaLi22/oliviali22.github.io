## Research question

What would a small, transparent research environment need in order to compare equity signals fairly across time and market conditions?

## Motivation

Quantitative research needs an auditable path from raw data to interpretation. This case study is a proposed direction for that workflow; it does not claim a tested signal or investment outcome.

## Methodology

**Planned:** define a research universe, establish point-in-time data rules, construct a small set of candidate factors or signals, and compare them through a consistent backtesting and reporting framework.

## Data and assumptions

No data vendor or universe has been chosen. Future documentation will state survivorship handling, corporate actions, timing, liquidity screens, and cost assumptions before presenting results.

## Implementation

**Planned:** modular Python and SQL components for ingesting and validating financial data, running factor-based research and backtests, and reporting results. The repository link will appear when code is available.

## Results and findings

No backtest or performance statistics are available. Future charts should show both results and the uncertainty around them.

## Limitations and next steps

Potential concerns include data quality, look-ahead bias, multiple testing, regime dependence, and the gap between research conditions and implementation.

Next: select a point-in-time data source, define a universe and validation protocol, then implement a small research workflow before presenting any findings.
